The First Official Ferrari // Chassis 01C

Ferrari 125 S — The Car That Started It All

On March 12, 1947, Enzo Ferrari took the wheel of the very first car bearing his own name on the historic road outside the Maranello factory gates. Engineered by the legendary Gioacchino Colombo, the 125 S was an uncompromised racing prototype powered by a compact, high-revving 1.5-litre 60° V12 engine.

Just two weeks after its competitive baptism at Piacenza, Franco Cortese drove the 125 S to Ferrari's first historic overall victory at the Rome Grand Prix on May 25, 1947, circling the ancient Baths of Caracalla for 40 laps at an average speed of 88.5 km/h.

Key Milestones In Scuderia History
1951 SILVERSTONE GP

First Formula 1 Victory

José Froilán González drives the 4.5L V12 Ferrari 375 F1 to victory at Silverstone, defeating Alfa Romeo and establishing Ferrari as the new titan of Grand Prix racing.

DRIVER: J. F. GONZÁLEZ FERRARI 375 F1
1952 — 1953 WORLD TITLES

Back-to-Back Championships

Alberto Ascari dominates the world in the Ferrari 500 F2, winning 9 consecutive Grands Prix—a record that stood untouched for over six decades.

DRIVER: ALBERTO ASCARI FERRARI 500 F2
1962 GT MOTORSPORT

The Legendary 250 GTO

Powered by a 300 HP 3.0L Colombo V12, the 250 GTO captured three consecutive FIA International Championships for GT Manufacturers, becoming the most revered car on earth.

HERITAGE: 36 PRODUCED 250 GTO
1975 — 1977 FLAT-12 REVOLUTION

The 312T Transverse Era

Mauro Forghieri's revolutionary transverse gearbox and 500 HP Flat-12 engine propelled Niki Lauda to two World Championships and secured three Constructors' Titles.

DRIVER: NIKI LAUDA FERRARI 312T
2000 — 2004 GOLDEN ERA

5 Consecutive Double Titles

Michael Schumacher and Scuderia Ferrari rewrite all record books. The 2004 F2004 with its screaming 3.0L V10 remains one of the most dominant racing machines in human history.
